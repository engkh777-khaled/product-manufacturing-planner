What-If Scenarios

A scenario changes a copy of today's plant data, plans it next to the unchanged data with the same solver, and saves the result as a separate version. The approved baseline v{{ baseline.version_number }} is never touched. Scenarios built on hypothetical facts (delay, urgent order, quantity, extra machine) are for comparison only and cannot be submitted for approval.
